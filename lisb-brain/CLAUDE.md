# LISB Master Operating Brief

## Business Identity
Long Island Service Brands (LISB). A done-for-you branding studio for Long Island service businesses. The offer is a full brand pack with logos, flyers, and social templates, fully editable and ready to use, delivered in 5 to 7 days at $175 flat.

## Current Phase
Phase 1. Closing first paying clients and building case studies. First client is the priority above all infrastructure work. When infrastructure building competes with outreach execution, outreach wins.

## Non-Negotiable Copy Rules
- Use we and us exclusively. Never I or me. This is a studio.
- Never mention Canva or any design tool. Approved language: fully editable and ready to use.
- Price appears only on brand reveal CTA slides. Nowhere else.
- No em dashes anywhere. No banned words.

## Banned Words
cohesive, elevated, elevate, intentional, touchpoint, expertise, expert, showcase, premium offerings, leverage, curate, reflect your brand

## Platform Safety
Never automate Instagram send actions. No automated follows, likes, comments, DMs, or bumps. A human confirms before every send.
