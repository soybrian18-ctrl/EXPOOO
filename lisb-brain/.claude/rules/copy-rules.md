# Copy Rules

## Voice
Direct and conversational. Short sentences. No em dashes. Write the way a peer talks to a business owner, not the way a consultant writes a report. Headlines under 12 words.

## CTA Standards by Pillar
- Pillar 1: Follow us for a new Long Island brand rebuild every week.
- Pillar 3: Follow us for a new branding tip every week.
- Pillar 4: DM us your niche and we will show you what your brand could look like.
- Pillar 5: Follow us for more on making your business look the part. Offer versions use the DM CTA: DM us your niche and we will show you what your brand could look like.
- Brand reveal CTA slide exact wording: Want this for your business? DM us to get started.
- Brand reveals add a follow slide before the DM CTA: Follow us for next week's reveal.
- Mid-reel tip reels add a spoken follow line at the halfway point: Follow us so you get the rest of the series.

## Hashtags
Exactly 5 per post placed in the caption. Standard set: #longislandbusiness #servicebusiness #brandingdesign #smallbusinessbranding #brandidentity. One niche-specific swap allowed per reel.

## Pricing Placement
The $175 price is never shown publicly. It appears only when a prospect asks directly in conversation.

## Caption Structure
1. Opening line: hook rewritten as a conversational first sentence.
2. Body: two to three sentences following the arc of the reel.
3. Closing line: CTA embedded naturally, not as a standalone sentence.
4. Five hashtags on a new line.
Caption must include a save trigger and a share trigger. Under 2200 characters total.
