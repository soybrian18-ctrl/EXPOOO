## Grid Visual Rhythm
- Standard content uses navy background with white text.
- Every third reel uses a gold background to break the pattern and stop the scroll.
- Med spa and aesthetic content can use a warm dark background for editorial register.
- Brand reveals show the design itself as the full visual with no text overlay on the first frame.

## Reel Thumbnails
The reel cover is chosen separately from frame one. The cover serves the profile
visitor browsing the grid, frame one serves the scroller, so a reveal keeps its
no-text-overlay opening and still carries a branded cover.

Fixed signature, identical on every cover, no exceptions:
- A gold hairline the full width with the LISB mark centered directly below it,
  locked to the same position and size every time.
- The same outer margin every time.
- Only Cormorant Garamond for the hook and DM Sans small caps for the kicker.
- Navy, white, and gold only. Client color never enters our framing.

Variable zone above the hairline:
- Ground. Navy by default, gold on every third reel per the grid rhythm above,
  warm dark for aesthetic niches.
- Kicker. DM Sans small caps, wide tracking. Names the value in owner language,
  never internal pillar terms.
- Hook. Cormorant Garamond, three to six words, maximum two lines, a compressed
  version of the reel's own hook.

Per pillar:
- Branding Tips: kicker BRAND TIP with a running number, which implies a series
  and drives follows.
- Brand Equity: kicker names the stake in owner terms, for example WHAT IT COSTS YOU.
- Business Mindset: no kicker, type only, the most whitespace.
- Brand Reveals: client mark or hero surface fills the frame, kicker is the brand
  name and niche, no hook line. The hairline and mark keep it in the system.
- The Rebuild: kicker THE REBUILD, ground stays navy every week.

Legibility:
- Keep the kicker, hook, hairline, and mark inside the central safe area, since grid
  crops change and the sides are lost first.
- High contrast only. White on navy, navy on gold. Never gold text on navy at small size.
- On busy reveal covers, the hairline and mark sit on a solid navy band.

## Addendum: Distinctive vs Default by Niche (researched August 2026)
Guides the builds of Ellis, Alder & Stone, and Eastlight, and sets the refinement
bar for Garrison and Marlowe once built. Addendum to the locked directions above,
not a replacement.

What reads expensive across every premium local-service niche:
- The application layer wins, not the logo. Art-directed photography, print
  objects shown in content, and one look carried across truck, sign, card, and
  feed separate expensive from generic more than any mark does.
- One signature element per brand, executed with restraint. A custom ligature, a
  drawn ampersand, a badge shape. One, not several.
- Type character over decoration. Premium is a distinctive typeface used with
  confidence and whitespace, not added ornament.
- Palette sits one register off the niche default so it reads considered.
- Durability ruling: we sell durability and pricing power, so every brand anchors
  in idioms that have read expensive for decades. Medical modernism, motor-club
  heritage, architectural signage, editorial masthead. The 2026 drift toward
  louder, funkier, maximalist logos is noise for our niches. Do not chase it.

Med spa. Default now: sage green with blush and gold, spindly script or thin
serif, line-art leaf and face marks, the Instagram med spa template. Distinctive
now: medical luxury, clinical credibility carried with warmth, serif and sans
pairing, palette chosen by positioning instead of wellness habit.
Ellis verdict: current and expensive. One flag: eucalyptus sage is the niche
default color. Porcelain and slate stay dominant, sage is the minor accent only,
and the wide-tracked wordmark carries the identity.

Auto detailing. Default now: black plus one neon, aggressive italic type, carbon
fiber texture, shield badges, LED-neon energy. Distinctive now: heritage craft,
the Meguiar's since-1901 and Swissvax register, restraint and provenance cues.
Garrison verdict: right register, one dating risk. The 2010s hipster badge with
banners, crossed tools, and est. dates would read dated. Execute as clean
enamel-badge geometry: no banners, no crossed tools, no est. date, and let bone
and oxblood carry the heritage.

Landscaping. Default now: forest green and brown, leaf clipart, rounded sans,
truck-wrap-first thinking. Distinctive now: branded like a landscape architecture
studio, editorial project photography, architectural type, materials-led palette.
Alder & Stone verdict: current and durable. The risk is anonymity, not
datedness, since minimal architectural wordmarks blur together. The elongated
ampersand is the signature, protect it, and lead every application with project
photography.

Boutique fitness and pilates. Default now: two defaults, the black-plus-neon
bootcamp look, and increasingly the warm beige minimalism most premium pilates
studios adopted together. Distinctive now: warm minimalism that carries a real
identifying mark and a deeper, less beige-centered palette.
Eastlight verdict: the one direction at genuine saturation risk. Warm minimalism
is the current pilates default center. Keep the direction but anchor it: black
olive leads, terracotta is the accent not the field, the rising horizon-line
mark and the type character do the identifying, and never beige-first layouts.

Wedding and portrait photography. Default now: script logo plus the word
Photography, blush and gold, hairline serif website templates. Distinctive now:
the editorial masthead register, refined serifs with classic proportions, print
objects as proof, restraint over ornament.
Marlowe verdict: current and timeless, the masthead idiom holds. Sharpen: the M
ligature must be genuinely custom to escape the template-serif wave, no foil or
metallic gradient effects, and the serif stays weighted enough to survive small
sizes on screen.
