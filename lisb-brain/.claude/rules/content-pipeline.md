# Content Pipeline

## Active Content Pillars
- Pillar 1: Brand Equity and Perceived Value
- Pillar 3: Branding Tips
- Pillar 4: Brand Reveals
- Pillar 5: Business Mindset
Pillar 2 is permanently removed.

## Reel Structure
7-slide format. Hook, 4 body slides with gold labels, closing statement slide, CTA slide.

## Hook Rule
Opens with a problem or a result. Never a question. Niche-specific. Under 10 words.

## Posting Schedule ET
- Tuesday 7 to 9 AM: Pillar 1 and Pillar 4
- Wednesday 11 AM to 1 PM: Pillar 3
- Thursday 7 to 9 AM: Pillar 5
- Sunday 6 to 8 PM: pure mindset, no offer

## Algorithm Scoring
Hook score minimum 7. All four checks must pass: hook strength, watch time under 45 seconds, save trigger found, share trigger found. If any check fails, rebuild before posting.

## Production Tools
- ElevenLabs Adam voice. Stability 0.80, similarity 0.90, speed 0.85.
- Remotion at 1080 by 1920. FADE at 6 frames. 45-frame tail buffer on last slide.
- Per-slide audio clips. Never one continuous audio block.
- ffmpeg for audio mixing.
- NCS.io for background music by genre and mood per niche.
