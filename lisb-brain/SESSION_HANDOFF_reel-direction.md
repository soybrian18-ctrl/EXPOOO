## Decisions made
- The Rebuild is the flagship acquisition format. Reveals are secondary proof content.
- No public price ever. The $175 appears only in direct conversation when asked. The
  preflight gate catches figures, not the topic of pricing, so educational content about
  pricing passes.
- Anchor brands are fictional portfolio concepts. Captions must open with portfolio
  framing and may never imply a real client or paid engagement. This is enforced by a
  documented preflight framing check.
- Durability over trend-chasing. Every brand anchors in an idiom that has read expensive
  for decades. The 2026 drift toward louder maximalist logos is noise for our niches.
- Thumbnails unify through a fixed gold hairline plus LISB mark signature that never
  moves, with ground, kicker, and hook varying above it. The reel cover is separate from
  frame one, so reveals keep their no-text-overlay opening and still carry branded covers.
- Voice register follows what the viewer is asked to do, not the topic. Reveals are
  underplayed, equity is level and serious, tips are brisk, mindset is warmest. Confident,
  never hyped, on every pillar.
- Voice: we and us only, owner-framed, customer and outcome language, never design terms,
  no banned words, no em dashes, no criticism-framing.
- Platform safety: every Instagram action is manual and human-confirmed. Loops only
  prepare and organize. The human sends.
