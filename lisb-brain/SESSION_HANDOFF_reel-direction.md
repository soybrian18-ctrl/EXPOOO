## Decisions made (stable)
- The Rebuild is the flagship acquisition format; reveals are secondary proof.
- No public price ever; the preflight gate catches figures, not the pricing topic;
  client-side pricing may appear on promo artwork.
- Anchor brands are fictional portfolio concepts; captions open with portfolio
  framing, enforced by the framing gate.
- Recognition before distinction on every niche build; the cover-the-name test
  gates production (references/niche-recognition-standard.md).
- Durability over trend-chasing; thumbnails unify through the fixed gold hairline
  plus mark signature; the reel cover is separate from frame one.
- Voice register follows what the viewer is asked to do (reveals underplayed,
  equity level, tips brisk, mindset warmest; confident, never hyped).
- We and us only, owner-framed, no banned words, no em dashes, no criticism
  framing. Platform safety: every Instagram action is manual and human-confirmed.
